Privacy Policy

Signalyze — WiFi Analyzer · Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Signalyze ("the App", "we", "us"), published by DTH Studio, handles information when you use the App. By using Signalyze you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information the App processes

Signalyze is a network-diagnostic tool. The Wi-Fi and network data it analyzes is processed on your device only and is not sent to us or stored on any server we control:

  • Wi-Fi & network information — nearby network names (SSID), signal strength, channel, frequency band, security type, and details of your current connection (IP address, gateway, DNS, link speed, MAC/BSSID). This information is read from the Android system, shown to you in the App, and is not transmitted off your device by us.
  • Location permission — Android requires the location permission to return Wi-Fi scan results. Signalyze uses this permission solely to read nearby Wi-Fi networks. The App does not collect, store, or share your geographic location.
  • Bluetooth scan permission (Android 12+, neverForLocation) — used only by the optional Bluetooth scan tool to list nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices on-screen. It is not used to derive your location, and results are never uploaded or stored.
  • No account — the App does not require sign-up and does not collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts.

The App shows banner ads (Google AdMob) and uses the Google Firebase services below (analytics and crash reporting), which collect limited technical and usage data as described.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the App free, Signalyze shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. The AdMob SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID, coarse/approximate location derived from your IP address, and general device/technical information (device model, OS version, network type) used to select, deliver and measure ads. AdMob does not receive your Wi-Fi scan results, network names (SSID), hardware addresses (BSSID), or saved surveys. This data is handled by Google under its Advertising policies. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Settings → Google → Ads.

3. Analytics & crash reporting

Signalyze uses Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics), provided by Google LLC, to keep the App stable and understand basic, aggregated usage so we can improve it. These SDKs may collect:

  • Crash & diagnostic data (Firebase Crashlytics) — stack traces, the device model and OS version, the app version, and diagnostic log breadcrumbs at the time of a crash. This is used only to find and fix bugs.
  • Usage & device data (Firebase Analytics) — app-usage events (such as which tools are opened), general device/OS information, coarse country/region derived from your IP address, and a pseudonymous app-instance identifier, used in aggregate to understand feature usage.

We do not upload your Wi-Fi scan results, SSIDs, or BSSID/MAC addresses to these services. By design, hardware addresses (BSSID/MAC) are never written to logs and therefore never reach Crashlytics. This data is handled by Google under its policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy. On devices without Google Play services these features are simply inactive.

4. Internet Speed Test (M-Lab) & other network tools

Signalyze includes an optional Internet Speed Test that runs only when you start it (by tapping "GO"). If you never use it, none of the following applies.

To measure your download speed, upload speed, latency (ping) and jitter, the App connects to the nearest public test server operated by Measurement Lab (M-Lab), an open-science research consortium, and exchanges test data with it for a few seconds.

Important — M-Lab publishes test results as open, public research data. For each test you run, the information M-Lab records and makes publicly available includes your device's public IP address and a coarse location derived from it, the server used, a timestamp, and the measured network metrics. It does not include your name, the app used, your Wi-Fi network name (SSID), or any hardware (MAC/BSSID) address. We do not send your scan results to M-Lab.

By running the speed test you accept M-Lab's collection and public release of this data. See M-Lab's privacy policy: https://www.measurementlab.net/privacy/. If you prefer not to share this, do not use the Internet Speed Test — the rest of the App works without it.

Similarly, the optional Public IP tool (inside Network tools) runs only when you tap it, and sends a request to a third-party IP-geolocation service (ipwho.is) to show your public IP address and its approximate city/ISP. The DNS and WHOIS tools likewise query public internet infrastructure. The MAC and Subnet tools are fully offline.

5. How information is used

  • To provide the App's core features (scanning, signal analysis, security checks, speed test).
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability and features (Firebase).

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Settings → Google → Ads — this also limits what Firebase Analytics can associate.
  • You can limit analytics tracking system-wide via the same Google settings, or by using a device without Google Play services (which disables Firebase entirely).
  • You can revoke the location, nearby-Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Signalyze → Permissions (some features will stop working).

7. Data retention

Wi-Fi and network data is held only in memory while the App is running and is not persisted to a remote server by us. Analytics and crash data are retained by Google, and Internet Speed Test results are published and retained by M-Lab, each under their respective policies. Any locally cached preferences, saved surveys and speed-test history stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.

8. Children's privacy

Signalyze is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
